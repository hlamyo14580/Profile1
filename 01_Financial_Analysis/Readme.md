📊 #** From Financial Reporting to Decision Support

I recently developed a Financial Performance & Analysis dashboard to bring together Finance, BI and Data Analytics into one management reporting framework.

Rather than asking only “What happened?”, I structured the analysis around four levels:

1️⃣ Descriptive Analytics — What happened?
• Executive KPIs
• Income Statement trends
• Balance Sheet position
• Cash Flow movements
• Regional performance

2️⃣ Diagnostic Analytics — Why did it happen?
• Actual vs Budget variance
• Revenue and profitability drivers
• Margin movement
• Cash conversion
• Regional contribution and performance gaps

3️⃣ Predictive Analytics — What could happen next?
• Historical trends can be extended into forecasting
• Scenario and sensitivity analysis can test potential revenue, margin and cash-flow outcomes
• Forecasting should be validated against business assumptions and data quality

4️⃣ Prescriptive Analytics — What should we consider doing?
• Prioritize regions with material performance gaps
• Investigate the drivers behind unfavorable budget variance
• Protect profitable revenue growth
• Monitor liquidity, leverage and cash conversion
• Translate financial signals into management actions

The key lesson for me is that a dashboard should not stop at displaying numbers.

The real value comes from connecting:
DATA → INSIGHT → EXPLANATION → OUTLOOK → ACTION

Tools and techniques used include Power BI, DAX, financial modelling, variance analysis, KPI design, management reporting and interactive data storytelling.

I’m continuing to strengthen my Finance + Data Analytics capability by building solutions that help stakeholders understand not only the numbers, but also the business questions behind them.
