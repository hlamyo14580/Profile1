🏗️ # **Construction Project Performance Dashboard | Power BI Executive Overview**

A Power BI project-performance solution designed to help project management monitor project progress, schedule performance, and budget utilization across a construction portfolio.

The objective was not only to visualize project data, but to answer the management questions:

Are projects progressing as expected?
Where are schedule risks emerging?
How much budget has been consumed?
Which areas require management attention?
What actions should be taken next?

1️⃣ **Project Detail**  
Business Question

How much work has been completed, and where should management focus?

Key Findings
The portfolio contains 4 projects and 10,000 tracked tasks.
Only 25.2% of tasks are completed.
50.3% remain uninitiated, representing the largest task category.
24.5% are currently active.
The project-level view provides visibility into different task-status patterns across projects.
Management Recommendation
Prioritize projects with a high volume of uninitiated tasks.
Review critical activities and upcoming milestones weekly.
Investigate whether resources are aligned with the remaining workload.
Escalate tasks that may affect downstream project activities.
Management Takeaway

The immediate priority is not simply increasing task completion—it is ensuring that the right tasks are started and completed in the right sequence.

2️⃣ **Project Timeline**  
Business Question

Are project activities progressing according to the planned timeline?

Key Findings
The timeline provides visibility into completed, in-progress, blocked and not-started activities.
Activities have different progress durations and statuses.
The timeline helps identify activities that may require closer monitoring before they affect downstream work.
Management Recommendation
Focus weekly reviews on blocked and delayed activities.
Monitor activities that sit on or influence the critical path.
Identify dependencies before starting downstream tasks.
Use the timeline as an early-warning mechanism rather than waiting for final project delays.
Management Takeaway

Schedule control should be proactive: identify activities that can create future delays before they become project-level problems.

3️⃣ **Project Budget**  
Business Question 

How much of the project budget has been consumed, and is spending aligned with project progress?

Key Findings
Total budget: $272.99M
Spending: $228.30M
Remaining balance: $44.69M
Budget utilization: 83.72%
Remaining budget: approximately 16.37%
Cost per completed task: $90.52K
Management Recommendation
Closely monitor remaining budget against remaining project workload.
Perform regular Budget vs Spending variance reviews.
Investigate projects where spending is high relative to physical progress.
Review major cost categories and spending trends before committing additional budget.
Management Takeaway

High budget utilization should be reviewed together with physical progress and remaining workload—not evaluated in isolation.

➡️ This allows management to investigate whether cost consumption is aligned with actual project progress.


🧠 Senior Data Analyst Perspective

The most important lesson from this project is:

A dashboard should not stop at “What happened?” It should help management answer “So what?” and “What should we do next?”


**My analytical approach**

1. Monitor →
Track project KPIs and operational status.

2. Identify →
Find unusual progress, schedule or spending patterns.

3. Investigate →
Drill into project, task and cost-level details.

4. Prioritize →
Focus management attention on high-impact risks.

5. Act →
Recommend resource, schedule or cost-control actions.

6. Monitor Again →
Track whether corrective actions improve performance.


📌 **Recommended Management Meeting Approach**

Instead of presenting every chart, I would brief the Project Director in this order:

1. Start with the headline

“We currently have 10,000 tracked tasks across 4 projects. 25.2% are completed, while 50.3% remain uninitiated.”


2. Explain the schedule position

“The timeline identifies activities requiring closer monitoring, particularly blocked or delayed work that could affect downstream activities.”


3. Explain financial exposure

“$228.30M of the $272.99M budget has been spent, representing 83.72% utilization.”


4. Connect the signals

“The key management question is whether remaining work can be completed within the remaining budget and planned schedule.”


5. Recommend action

“I recommend prioritizing high-impact uninitiated/blocked activities, reviewing resource allocation, and conducting tighter budget-versus-progress monitoring.”


💡 **Final Business Wisdom**

Project performance is not one KPI.

A strong project-control view connects:

Progress → Schedule → Cost → Risk → Action

The value of this Power BI solution is therefore not simply reporting project numbers.

It provides management with a structured way to:

See the problem → Understand the impact → Prioritize the response → Monitor the outcome.
